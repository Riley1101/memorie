What changed in each release, newest first. Downloads and checksums are on GitHub Releases.

0.3.318 November 2026

A new look, and room for the whole book

New

  • A new look. A translucent window, calmer colours with one warm accent, and muted binder colours. Every screen was redesigned, including the library, Settings and the codex.
  • Margin notes. Select any text and leave yourself a note beside it. Notes stay in place as you edit, travel with the writing, and never appear in an export.
  • A fuller scene panel. A scene’s status, synopsis and margin notes now sit together beside the page.
  • Mentions in the codex. Open an entry to see every sentence that mentions it, grouped by writing.
  • Your own order. Drag writings into the order you want. Export follows that order, not the alphabet.
  • Recently deleted. Deleted writings and folders can be restored for 30 days.
  • Compare with an earlier version. From History, compare the page with any saved version. Changes are marked word by word, and you can keep or undo each one.
  • A welcome tour. New writers get a short welcome writing that shows the command palette, binders, the codex and history.

Improved

  • The assistant opens as a panel beside the page, and you can drag it wider. It only speaks when you ask.
  • The ⌘K palette and command bar were rebuilt to match the new look.
  • A clearer export dialog.
  • Rename writings in place, without the list jumping.
  • The binder list shows on laptop screens.
  • Deleting from Recently deleted forever asks first.
  • If a save fails, the message stays and offers a retry. The save status is quieter the rest of the time.
  • Easier to read: no text below 12px, and lines capped at about 66 characters.
  • Every icon button has a name for screen readers, and shortcut labels are correct.

Faster

  • Typing stays smooth in manuscripts of 500,000 words. The editor skips work on pages you can’t see and saves only the parts that changed.
  • Long books open faster, and saving no longer pauses the page.

Fixed

  • On macOS, Memoire asks for keychain access once per session, not on every sync or assistant request.
  • Deleted writings are kept out of your GitHub sync repository.
  • The drop cap no longer disappears while you type.
  • Fixed several places where an unexpected error could close the app.
  • Updated dependencies to fix known security issues.

0.3.116 October 2026

Automatic updates

New

  • Memoire checks for updates in the background and offers a restart when one is ready.
  • Settings → Updates shows your version and lets you check by hand.
  • Every update is signed, and the app refuses one it can’t verify.

Improved

  • The command bar scrolls to keep the selected suggestion in view.
0.3.027 September 2026

Codex, find and replace, and a rebuilt export

New

  • A codex for characters, locations, factions and items. Memoire finds each entry in your writing and the assistant gets the relevant sheets.
  • Find and replace with match case, whole word and regex.
  • Export to PDF, Word or EPUB with a title page, chapter outline and table of contents.
  • Smart punctuation: curly quotes, dashes and ellipses as you type.
  • Auto-paired brackets and a link popover.

Faster

  • Quicker launch and note indexing.
  • AI runs faster on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs, with better search results.
  • Undo history uses less memory in long sessions.
0.2.023 September 2026

Dropbox sync

New

  • Sync your writing to a Dropbox folder. If a file changed in both places, both copies are kept.
  • Choose GitHub or Dropbox as your preferred storage.

Fixed

  • Renaming inside a nested chapter no longer moves the writing.
  • Case-only renames now work on macOS and Windows.
  • Grammar suggestions replace the right paragraph after edits.
0.1.018 September 2026

First public release

New

  • A distraction-free editor with a live outline, goals and a command palette.
  • Binders with nested chapters and drag-and-drop.
  • Branching undo history and Git sync.
