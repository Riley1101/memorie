Privacy policy

Memoire is a writing app that runs on your computer. This page says what it stores, when it connects to the internet, and what leaves your device when it does.

In short
  • There is no account, and we collect no analytics, telemetry or crash reports.
  • Your writing is saved as plain files on your computer. We never receive it.
  • Anything that sends your writing elsewhere (sync, hosted AI) is off until you turn it on, and goes only to the service you pick.

What stays on your computer

Everything Memoire makes is stored on your device:

  • Your writing, as Markdown files in ~/.memorie/content, including codex sheets and scene details.
  • Version history, the search index and settings, elsewhere in ~/.memorie.
  • Downloaded AI models, if you turn on local AI.
  • Logs, which help with bug reports. They are written locally and only leave your computer if you attach them to a report yourself.

Access tokens and API keys are kept in your operating system's secure store: Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows, and the Secret Service on Linux. They are never sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. Memoire has no analytics, no advertising identifiers, no usage tracking and no crash reporting. We do not run a server that the app reports to.

When Memoire connects to the internet

The app works fully offline. It only connects in the cases below. Each service receives your IP address and the request itself, as with any connection, and handles it under its own privacy policy.

If you install Memoire from the Microsoft Store, the Store handles updates and the app's own update check is not used. Microsoft's privacy statement covers the Store itself.

Opening a web link from your writing hands it to your default browser.

Third-party services

When you turn on one of the features above, that service's own policy applies to what it receives:

  • GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Dropbox Privacy Policy
  • OpenRouter Privacy Policy
  • Hugging Face Privacy Policy

Deleting your data

Because everything is on your device, you are in control of it:

  • Delete ~/.memorie/content to remove your writing, or the whole ~/.memorie folder to also remove history, settings and models.
  • Uninstalling the app leaves that folder in place, so your writing is not lost by accident.
  • Disconnect GitHub, Dropbox or OpenRouter in Settings to remove the stored token or key. You can also revoke access from that service's own settings.

Files you have synced stay in your GitHub repository or Dropbox folder until you delete them there.

Children

Memoire does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If a release changes what the app sends or stores, this page and the changelog will say so, and the date at the top will change.

Questions about privacy: open an issue on GitHub, or see Support.
