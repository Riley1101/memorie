Support

Memoire is built in the open, so help happens on GitHub. Most questions are answered below.

Report a bugSay what you did, what you expected, and your app version. A screenshot helps. Search known issuesSee if someone has already reported it, and follow the fix.

Find your version in Settings → Updates. We read every report, usually within a few days.

Common questions

Where is my writing saved?

In ~/.memorie/content in your home folder, as plain Markdown files. Settings shows the exact path under Storage. You can open the files in any text editor.

How do I back up my writing?

Connect GitHub or Dropbox in Settings → Cloud Sync. With GitHub, every sync becomes a commit you can go back to. You can also copy the content folder to any backup drive.

How do I move to a new computer?

Sync to GitHub or Dropbox, install Memoire on the new computer, connect the same account, and pull. Or copy the ~/.memorie folder across.

How do I turn on the assistant?

Open Settings → AI. Choose a local model to run on your computer, or add your own OpenRouter key to use a hosted model. AI stays off until you do.

How do I update the app?

Memoire checks for updates on its own and offers a restart when one is ready. You can also check in Settings → Updates. Copies from the Microsoft Store update through the Store.

macOS says the app is damaged. What do I do?

Mac builds aren't notarized yet, so macOS blocks them the first time. Move Memoire to Applications, then run this once in Terminal:

xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/memoire.app
Does Memoire collect any data?

No. There is no account and no analytics. The privacy policy lists every case where the app connects to the internet.

Is it really free?

Yes. Memoire is open source under the MIT License, with no paid tier.
